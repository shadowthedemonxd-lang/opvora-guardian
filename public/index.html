    Deadly look-alikes

    The fake is one character away from the real thing. These come straight from the scanner's own examples and the console feed.

    1. Realbanconova.com.mx
      Fakebancon0va-secure-login.top

      A zero for the “o”, security words bolted on, and a .top ending. The real bank uses banconova.com.mx.

    2. Realpaypal.com
      Fakepaypal.com.account-review.xyz

      It starts with paypal.com, but the real domain is whatever comes last: account-review.xyz.

    3. Realinvoice_8812.pdf
      Fakeinvoice_8812.pdf.exe

      It looks like a PDF. The .exe at the very end makes it a program that runs.

    How a verdict is made

    Two independent layers look at every item. When they disagree, the stricter one wins.

    1. Collect

      An alert arrives from the browser, the PC or an account, or you paste something into the scanner.

      kind: message105 chars

    2. Rule engine

      29 deterministic checks catch known tricks in milliseconds: look-alike domains, encoded commands, threats, demands for your ID.

      threat +35pii-demand +25deadline +10

    3. AI analyst

      The model weighs context the rules can't, scores the risk from 0 to 100 and writes the explanation.

      risk 95severity: critical

    4. Reconcile

      The server checks the answer for contradictions. Strong rule hits set a floor the AI can't argue below.

      rules 70 → floor: highverdict: Critical

    The tags under each step trace Plate I, the threatening message, through the pipeline. Rule weights are the engine's real values.
