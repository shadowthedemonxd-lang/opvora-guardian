    How it works

    Two layers of judgment, one clear answer.

    1. 01

      Collect

      Events arrive from the browser, the PC and connected accounts. In this demo they are scripted samples.

    2. 02

      Rule engine

      Fast, deterministic checks catch known tricks: look-alike domains, raw IPs, encoded commands, mass file renames.

    3. 03

      AI analyst

      Llama 3.3 70B on Cloudflare Workers AI weighs the context, sets severity and writes the explanation.

    4. 04

      Explain and act

      You get a plain-language verdict, the red flags it saw and the steps to take, most urgent first.
