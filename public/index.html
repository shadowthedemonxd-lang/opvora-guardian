    How a verdict is made

    Two independent layers look at every item. When they disagree, the stricter one wins.

    1. Collect

      An alert arrives from the browser, the PC or an account, or you paste something into the scanner.

    2. Rule engine

      29 deterministic checks catch known tricks in milliseconds: look-alike domains, encoded commands, threats, demands for your ID.

    3. AI analyst

      The model weighs context the rules can't, scores the risk from 0 to 100 and writes the explanation.

    4. Reconcile

      The server checks the answer for contradictions. Strong rule hits set a floor the AI can't argue below.
