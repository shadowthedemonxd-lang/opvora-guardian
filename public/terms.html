What OPVORA is

OPVORA gives an opinion on how risky a link, a message or an alert looks, and suggests what to do next. The opinion comes from a rule engine and an AI model. It can be wrong in both directions: it can miss a scam, and it can flag something harmless.

What it is not

  • It is not a substitute for your bank, the police or a professional. If you are in danger, call 911. For online threats and extortion in Mexico, call 088.
  • It is not connected to your device, browser or accounts, and it cannot block, remove or recover anything for you.

Using it fairly

  • Check things you received or need to judge. Don't paste other people's private data unless you have to, and never passwords or codes.
  • Don't use OPVORA to harass anyone, to test attacks against others, or to overload the service. Requests are rate limited.

What beta means

  • Features can change, break or be removed, and the service can be unavailable at times.
  • Your history lives in your browser. If you clear it or switch devices, it is gone.

No warranty

OPVORA is provided as it is, without any guarantee. As far as the law allows, the OPVORA team is not responsible for decisions made from its results or for losses that follow them. Before you act on something important, confirm it through an official channel you reach yourself.

Changes

These terms will change as OPVORA grows, especially when accounts arrive. The date at the top shows the current version. See also the privacy page.
